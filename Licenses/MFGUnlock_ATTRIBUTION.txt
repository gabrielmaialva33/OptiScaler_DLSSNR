Ada MFG unlock: finding the driver's DLSS-G snippet
===================================================

OptiScaler/framegen/dlssg/MfgUnlock.cpp and OptiScaler/hooks/LibraryLoad_Hooks.cpp
recognise the DLSS-G snippet NVIDIA's driver keeps in its over-the-air store,

    C:\ProgramData\NVIDIA\NGX\models\dlssg\versions\<n>\files\<hash>.bin

and unlock it as they unlock a game's own nvngx_dlssg.dll. No source is copied;
the rule (a module under \models\dlssg\ is a DLSS-G provider) is rewritten
against OptiScaler's own load hook.

Where the rule comes from:

  KleberMotta/OptiScaler-DLSS5-MFG-RTX40, commit 74c3bac,
  OptiScaler/mfgunlock/MfgUnlock_Patches.cpp (HasKnownDlssgPath)
  https://github.com/KleberMotta/OptiScaler-DLSS5-MFG-RTX40

  reached through janblade/OptiScaler-F5-DLSSNR-Multipass (cd826dc1) and
  wilsjo2/OptiScaler-DLSSNR-PreSR-Multipass (bf91eebd), which ported it.

That code is, by its own account, a port of the "MFG Unlock" ReShade addon:

  https://github.com/ImDreamt/MFGAdaUnlock-RenoDx   (original addon by Dreamt)
  https://github.com/mavismmg/MFGAdaUnlock-RenoDx   (fork maintained by mavismmg)

whose technique originates from dashdogy's RTX40MFG-Unlock:

  https://github.com/dashdogy/RTX40MFG-Unlock
