Measure detail (in-game detail and stability stats)
=====================================================

Project:  OptiScaler fork / DLSS-NR scene calibration by janblade
Source:   janblade commits 28daeebf and daeb388f
Licence:  GNU General Public License v3.0, the licence of OptiScaler itself

What is derived from it:
The detail measurement and scene calibration compute pass structure:
  * The 64x64 reduction grid layout (4 float4 texels per tile) and CPU-side
    hierarchical tile reduction math (`ReduceGrid`);
  * The fine-band detail frequency extraction, output-vs-input difference,
    raw high-frequency energy, frame-to-frame temporal stability (flicker)
    measurement, chroma saturation ratio, warmth balance, and shadow darkening;
  * The D3D12 ring readback buffer architecture with asynchronous CPU mapping
    avoiding pipeline stalls.
