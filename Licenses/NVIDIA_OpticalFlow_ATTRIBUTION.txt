NVIDIA Optical Flow API headers
===============================

Project:  NVIDIA Optical Flow SDK interface, API 5.0, as redistributed by dxvk-nvapi
Source:   https://github.com/jp7677/dxvk-nvapi (inc/nvofapi), commit 5c7b8b28c1657b8eccb1ca71d377de6ecc47cc25

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The NVIDIA Optical Flow motion source in OptiScaler/shaders/synth_motion/
(SynthMotionNvofa_Dx12.cpp, [DlssNr] SynthMotionSource=nvofa) is compiled
against two unmodified headers, vendored under OptiScaler/include/nvofa/:

  * nvOpticalFlowCommon.h
  * nvOpticalFlowD3D12.h

Nothing else is taken from NVIDIA. nvofapi64.dll itself is not shipped: it is
loaded at runtime from the NVIDIA driver on Windows, or from dxvk-nvapi under
Proton. The two shader passes (synth_motion_nvofa_prep.hlsl and
synth_motion_nvofa_expand.hlsl) are OptiScaler's own.
