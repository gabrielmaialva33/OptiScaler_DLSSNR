optimizer-fps-dlss5
===================

Project:  optimizer-fps-dlss5 by Yuri Grib (BeliyG3)
Source:   https://github.com/BeliyG3/optimizer-fps-dlss5 (commit 3636e6d8)

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The carry of DLSS-NR's model cadence, in
OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/precompile/dlssnr_cadence_rule.h and
OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/precompile/dlssnr_cadence.hlsl, is taken from its temporal
mode (core/shaders/temporal_mapping.hlsli, temporal_reproject.hlsli and
temporal_residual.hlsli): the depth mismatch with its 0..1 depth term and the best of
four texels around the reprojected point; the colour gate on chromaticity and luma;
the motion chain summed over the skipped frames, with each step validated and restarted
from the frame's own vector when its end shows another surface; the 9-tap acceptance
gated by the centre pixel's own result; the fill from the coarse residual scaled by the
luma ratio, the four rotated rings of same-depth taps, the "no match, no fill" rule and
the fill's luma applied along the pixel's own chroma; the clamped Catmull-Rom read of
the residual; and the chain handed to the model as its motion vectors, off the picture
where it is broken.

What is not theirs: the scheduling, the refusals and the forced-frame rules, the edge
ramp that spares still pixels by the border, the scaling of taps and rings with the
working size, the point-resampled depth snapshot, the plain box-averaged coarse residual,
and the resource lifetime. Their cross-pass blend, phase-in, Lucas-Kanade refinement,
expected depth, older passes, cells, compose pass and background queue are not ported.
See OptiScaler/dlssnr/design/model-cadence.md.
