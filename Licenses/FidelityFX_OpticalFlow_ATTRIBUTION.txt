FidelityFX optical flow
=======================

Project:  AMD FidelityFX SDK (FSR SDK), version 2.3.0, commit 60f4ea8
Source:   https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/FidelityFX-SDK

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The synthesized-motion estimator in OptiScaler/shaders/synth_motion/ runs the
FidelityFX optical flow, compiled into OptiScaler.dll:

  * The shader code under shaders/synth_motion/precompile/ffx/ is copied
    unchanged from the SDK: the FidelityFX core headers (ffx_core*.h,
    ffx_common_types.h), the single-pass downsampler (spd/ffx_spd.h) and the
    optical flow headers (opticalflow/ffx_opticalflow_*.h).

  * synth_motion_callbacks.h and the seven synth_motion_*.hlsl passes that wrap
    them (prepare luma, luminance pyramid, scene-change histogram and
    divergence, search, filter, scale) are derived from the SDK's
    ffx_opticalflow_callbacks_hlsl.h and ffx_opticalflow_*_pass.hlsl. Resource
    bindings, the root signature and the colour read were changed; the
    algorithms were not.

  * The host sequence in SynthMotion_Dx12.cpp (resource sizes, pyramid
    ping-pong, dispatch order and dimensions) follows the SDK's
    ffx_opticalflow.cpp.

Every one of those source files is listed under the MIT licence in the SDK's
docs/license.md. The rest of that SDK is distributed under a different, binary-
only licence, and none of it is compiled into OptiScaler.

What is not derived from it
---------------------------

synth_motion_expand.hlsl (8x8-block vectors to one vector per pixel) and
synth_motion_clear.hlsl are OptiScaler's own.
