DLSS5-Feeder
============

Project:  DLSS5-Feeder by Jean-Laurent Rouzies
Source:   https://github.com/jlrouzies-fr/DLSS5-Feeder

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The output stabilizer in OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/precompile/dlssnr_stabilizer.hlsl
and OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/DlssNr_Stabilizer_Dx12.cpp is taken from Feeder's
src/feed_hold12.h (v1.17): the anchor-based gate -- a 3x3 box of the input compared
against the box as it was when the pixel last moved, relative to local brightness,
with a soft edge from the tolerance to twice it -- and the hold/creep it drives.

What is not theirs: the quantisation-aware minimum step in the creep, the linear
luminance weights, the route and reset rules, and the resource lifetime. See
OptiScaler/dlssnr/design/output-stabilizer.md.
