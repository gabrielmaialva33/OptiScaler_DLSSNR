NR model loader and NGX loader scan
===================================

Two pieces of OptiScaler's Neural Rendering module rest on other projects'
work. Neither copies their source; both are rewritten against OptiScaler's own
structures. The design is recorded in OptiScaler/dlssnr/FORWARDER_INVESTIGATION.md
and OptiScaler/dlssnr/design/ngx-driver-616.md.


1. The direct model loader ([DlssNr] ModelLoader=direct)
--------------------------------------------------------

Project:  OptiScaler-DLSSNR-PreSR-Multipass, by wilsjo2
Source:   https://github.com/wilsjo2/OptiScaler-DLSSNR-PreSR-Multipass
          releases v0.8.1-nr-direct-runtime and v0.8.3,
          branch codex/nr-direct-runtime, commit 47e134cb
Licence:  GNU General Public License v3.0, the licence of OptiScaler itself

What is derived from it: the technique by which OptiScaler.dll calls
nvngx_dlssnr.dll without the nvngx.dll_dlssnr.dll forwarder. The model's
GetModuleFileNameW and GetModuleFileNameA import slots are found by name in the
loaded image, not by offset. They are pointed at an adapter that answers with an
nvngx.dll path only for the calling OptiScaler module and only during a direct
call, and that chains every other query to whatever the slot held before,
loader and overlay wrappers included.

Files: OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_DirectRuntime.h/.cpp and the import scan in
OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_PeScan.h.


2. The NGX loader's feature-18 route, and the model it faults with
------------------------------------------------------------------

Project:  dlss5-bridge, by NIGos
Source:   https://github.com/NIGos/dlss5-bridge
Licence:  MIT

What is derived from it:

  * The finding that NVIDIA's NGX loader (_nvngx.dll) from 32.0.16.1664 routes
    NGX feature 18 into nvngx_dlssnr.dll itself, and that model 310.8.0.0
    faults inside D3D12 on that route.
  * The layout of the loader's 19-entry feature-id -> snippet-name table, and
    the pattern that finds it (entry 1 "dlss", entry 11 "dlssg", entry 18
    "dlssnr" or ""), from PatchNgxFeatureTable. OptiScaler only reads the
    table, never writes it.
  * The version predicate for a model known to fault on that route
    (NrSnippetIsOlderThanLoader).

Files: the table scan and ModelKnownToFaultOnLoaderRoute in
OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_PeScan.h, used by OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_NgxInfo.cpp
and OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNr_Proxy.cpp.
