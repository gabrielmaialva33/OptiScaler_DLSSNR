PeripheralWarp
==============

Project:  optimizer-fps-dlss5 (PeripheralWarp, ABI v2) by Yuri Grib (BeliyG3)
Source:   https://github.com/BeliyG3/optimizer-fps-dlss5, commit 64902dd6a02460e5f6b778504ec2a4005faf4d9c
Through:  wilsjo2's OptiScaler-DLSSNR-PreSR-Multipass v0.8.91, external/peripheral_warp/SpatialWarp.h
          (https://github.com/wilsjo2/OptiScaler-DLSSNR-PreSR-Multipass, GPL-3.0)

What is derived from it
-----------------------

Peripheral compression's mapping between the frame and the model's packed input:
the rational radius curve and its exact inverse, the per-side pack and unpack around
the centre band, and the per-axis budget split (BuildAxis), in
OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/precompile/dlssnr_periphery_warp.h and
OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/DlssNr_Periphery.h. The logic is theirs line for line.

What is not theirs: the extent and validity rules ported from wilsjo2's
DlssNr_Spatial.h (GPL-3.0), the compression floor, the exact area filter of the
colour pack, the motion conversion in frame pixels, the unpack onto the uniform grid
and everything about how the pass runs. See
OptiScaler/dlssnr/design/peripheral-compression.md.
